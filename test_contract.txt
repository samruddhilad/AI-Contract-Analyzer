RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on January 1, 2024, between John Doe ("Landlord") and Jane Smith ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES: Landlord leases to Tenant the property located at 123 Maple Street.
2. TERM: The term of this lease begins on February 1, 2024, and lasts for a duration of 12 months, ending on January 31, 2025.
3. RENT: Tenant agrees to pay monthly rent of $1,500.00 due on the first day of each calendar month.
4. TERMINATION: Either party may terminate this agreement early by providing 30 days prior written notice. If Tenant leaves before the term ends without cause, Tenant forfeits the security deposit of $1,500.00.
5. GOVERNING LAW: This Agreement shall be governed by and construed under the laws of the State of California.
6. CONFIDENTIALITY: The terms of this lease are confidential between the parties.
